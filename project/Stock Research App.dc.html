1 Watchlist app — dark blueprint · v1 Industry design system, inverted to a dark ground
DESIGN NOTES
Navigation: one scrolling home, detail pushed on top

No tab bar. Home scrolls Movers → Today's digest → Watchlist table. Tapping any ticker anywhere pushes the detail screen; the digest card opens the digest timeline. Back is the Android gesture / header chevron — a two-level stack, nothing to get lost in.

Score visualisation

Concentric dual ring: outer = technical composite (−3…+7, filled from the −3 origin), inner = fundamentals percentile vs same-industry peers. Same glyph at 3 sizes — 26px in table rows, 34px in mover cards, 92px with numeric read-out on detail. On detail it expands into the signals that produced it.

Colour discipline

Industry is a mono steel system, so gain/loss is carried by contrast, not by red/green — with one stated deviation: the falling triangle is a dark red #c2503f, the only non-steel colour in the design. Up = bright steel #b5d9fd with ▲; on a down move the figure itself stays dimmed neutral #b7b7ba and only the ▼ turns red. Direction therefore reads from glyph, sign and contrast together, which survives colour-blind and bright-sunlight cases.

ASSUMPTIONS — CORRECT ME
· 38 tickers → one flat table, no sector grouping or virtual scroll needed. Sector is a filter chip instead.
· fundamentals_score is rendered as a 0–100 peer percentile; if it's actually a −3…+7 style scale I'll mirror the technical ring.
· Prices are 20-min delayed; the header carries a live "updated 4m ago" stamp so stale data is never ambiguous.
· No buy/sell, no watchlist editing in v1 — the watchlist is owned by the pipeline.
· v2 push alerts get a bell in the header and an alert row style in the digest timeline; stubbed, not designed here.
